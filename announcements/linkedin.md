💬 We built Lemma, a formal language for organizational constructs on the Lean 4 proof assistant. Every construct is written from a small vocabulary of basic parts at a stated level of analysis with a stated time horizon. A compiler reports constructs sharing parts, propositions with an undeclared scope, places where theories disagree, as well as relationships implied by existing claims yet untested.

🔑 Key findings
👉 On 67 papers covering job demands, engagement, burnout, plus thriving, the compiler reported exhaustion with vigor as built on a single underlying part, energy, a conclusion the field reached only after a decade of debate.
👉 A direct contradiction surfaced between two models of burnout.
👉 Composition of existing propositions yielded 85 relationships awaiting a first test.
👉 Three of nine papers were refused, with the missing piece named in each case.

💡 Conclusions
Inspired by AI progress in mathematics, where a shared library plus a mechanical checker let models learn from millions of attempts, our goal is to emulate the success AI has had for mathematics in organization and management science theory.
