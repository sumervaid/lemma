1/ We built Lemma, a formal language for organizational constructs on the Lean 4 proof assistant. Every construct is written from a small vocabulary of basic parts at a stated level of analysis with a stated time horizon.

2/ A compiler reports constructs sharing parts, propositions with an undeclared scope, places where theories disagree, as well as relationships implied by existing claims yet untested.

3/ On 67 papers covering job demands, engagement, burnout, plus thriving, the compiler reported exhaustion with vigor as built on a single underlying part, energy, a conclusion the field reached only after a decade of debate.

4/ A direct contradiction surfaced between two models of burnout. Composition of existing propositions yielded 85 relationships awaiting a first test. Three of nine papers were refused, with the missing piece named in each case.

5/ Inspired by AI progress in mathematics, our goal is to emulate the success AI has had for mathematics in organization and management science theory. Code, library, reports at https://github.com/sumervaid/lemma
