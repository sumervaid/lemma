TITLE: The measurement of experienced burnout
REFERENCE: Maslach, C. & Jackson, S. E. (1981). Journal of Occupational Behaviour, 2, 99-113.

DEFINITIONS
- Burnout: a syndrome of emotional exhaustion and cynicism that occurs frequently among individuals who do people-work.
- Emotional exhaustion: feelings of being emotionally overextended and exhausted by one's work.
- Depersonalization: an unfeeling and impersonal response toward recipients of one's service, care or treatment.
- Reduced personal accomplishment: a decline in one's feelings of competence and successful achievement in one's work with people.

PROPOSITIONS
1. The three subscales are distinct factors (factor analysis across samples).
2. Burnout relates to turnover and absenteeism (correlations with intention to leave).

MEASUREMENT: Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI), self-report, frequency and intensity scales; cross-sectional samples of human-service workers; individual level.
