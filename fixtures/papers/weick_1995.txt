TITLE: Sensemaking in Organizations
REFERENCE: Weick, K. E. (1995). Sage.

DEFINITIONS
- Sensemaking: an ongoing, retrospective, social process grounded in identity construction, enactive of sensible environments, focused on and by extracted cues, driven by plausibility rather than accuracy (seven properties, chapter 2).

PROPOSITIONS
The book advances no directional propositions between measured constructs. It describes properties of a process and illustrates them with cases (the Mann Gulch fire, Union Carbide Bhopal).

MEASUREMENT: none proposed; the work is interpretive.
