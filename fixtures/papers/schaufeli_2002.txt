TITLE: The measurement of engagement and burnout: a two sample confirmatory factor analytic approach
REFERENCE: Schaufeli, W. B., Salanova, M., González-Romá, V. & Bakker, A. B. (2002). Journal of Happiness Studies, 3, 71-92.

DEFINITIONS
- Work engagement: a positive, fulfilling, work-related state of mind characterized by vigor, dedication, and absorption (p. 74).
- Vigor: high levels of energy and mental resilience while working, willingness to invest effort, persistence (p. 74).
- Dedication: a sense of significance, enthusiasm, inspiration, pride, and challenge (p. 74).
- Absorption: being fully concentrated and deeply engrossed in one's work (p. 75).
- Burnout (MBI-GS): exhaustion, cynicism, reduced professional efficacy.

PROPOSITIONS
1. Engagement and burnout are negatively related but distinct constructs (two-factor model fits better than one-factor).
2. Engagement comprises three correlated factors (vigor, dedication, absorption).

MEASUREMENT: Utrecht Work Engagement Scale (UWES), self-report, frequency anchors; samples of students and employees; cross-sectional confirmatory factor analysis at the individual level.
